<objective>
Prepare the agenda for the store switch with one behavior-preserving step on the legacy data path: put every bookmark and filter write behind ViewModel intents.

Purpose: 09-09 must be a small commit a human can reason about and revert. Moving the views onto intents now means that commit touches no menu, and no detail or list view body. The intents take `(type:value:)` so that `.room` resolves against the Swift `FilterType` today and against the Kotlin `SessionFilterType` after the switch, with no change to the menu files.
Output: ViewModel intents, five menus and the detail view on those intents.
</objective>

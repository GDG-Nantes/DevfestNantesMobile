<objective>
Bump Compose BOM (D-01 stage 3), then migrate Apollo GraphQL to the 5.x line including the normalized-cache artifact-group move (D-01 stage 4) — two separate commits, in that order.

Purpose: 02-RESEARCH.md's second major finding is that Apollo Kotlin 5's cache story is not a same-package version bump. The two normalized-cache artifacts are deprecated in v5 in favour of an entirely new artifact group, `com.apollographql.cache`, published from a separate repository with its own version line and its own Apollo-compiler-plugin dependency. Bumping only the `appollo` version string leaves the two cache entries pointing at deprecated artifacts; the build may still resolve, and the app then runs a no-longer-wired cache implementation — a silent failure, not a loud one. The group change plus the compiler-plugin registration is therefore the bulk of this stage's work, and the destructive SQLite format migration it implies is exactly what D-06 pre-accepted.

Output: two revertible commits (Compose BOM; Apollo 5 + cache-package migration), both green on `android.yml` and `ios.yml`, with the cache mechanism functionally confirmed per D-07.
</objective>

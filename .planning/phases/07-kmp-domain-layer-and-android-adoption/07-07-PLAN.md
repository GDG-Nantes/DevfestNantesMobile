## Discretion decisions taken in this plan

- **Pre-characterization changes are limited to two items** (D-10, D-11, checker round 2):
  1. **D-10 extraction of the AgendaView filter code** (explicitly allowed by D-10): `AgendaView.sections(_ sections: [AgendaContent.Section], forDay day: String) -> [AgendaContent.Section]` (the `$0.day.contains(day)` filter of `filteredSections(for:)`) and `AgendaView.filteredSessions(_ sessions: [AgendaContent.Session], filters: Set<SessionFilter>, favorites: [String]) -> [AgendaContent.Session]` (body of `getFilteredSessions` followed by the favorites step of `filteredSessionsForSection`). `filteredSections(for:)` and `filteredSessionsForSection` delegate with `viewModel.agendaContent.sections`, `viewModel.sessionFilters` and `viewModel.favorites`; `getFilteredSessions` is deleted because it became a pure duplicate.
  2. **Access-only seam on the grouping**: `private func sessionsChanged(sessions: [Session])` becomes `func sessionsChanged(sessions: [Session])` (internal, reachable through `@testable import DevFest_Nantes`). The body, `getDate`, `parseDate` and `sectionDateFormatter` are not touched. This is not a logic move: the code 07-08 pins is the v1.0 grouping.
- **Not touched in Phase 7**: `SpeakersViewModel.getAlphabets()` / `speakers(for:)` and `VenueViewModel.currentLanguage` are already internal and reachable from a test through their existing initializers (`BaseViewModel.init(store:)` defaults to `IosDependencies.shared.store()`, and the hosted test runs after the app called the idempotent `IosDependencies.shared.start()`), so they need no change. The navigable check is a local expression inside the private `agendaRow` ViewBuilder: it cannot be called from a test without moving it, so it is not moved in Phase 7 (the iOS app keeps its own code until Phase 9, phase boundary); its iOS characterization belongs to the Phase 9 plan that replaces it (D-11 applies there), and the shared table is pinned by `ClassifySessionUseCaseTest` (07-01), equal to Android's `MainActivity` condition.
